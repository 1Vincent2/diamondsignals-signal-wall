WAVEFORM COLOR SYSTEM RED = DECAY / FATIGUE RISK LIME = EMERGENCE / POWER GAIN CYAN = MECHANICAL INSTABILITY
READING THE TRACE: color and shape are two different signals. Color shows the dominant kinetic diagnosis. Shape shows whether the last 3 outings are accelerating, cooling, or holding.
COLOR = SIGNAL FAMILY Red flags decay / fatigue risk. Lime flags emergence / power gain. Cyan flags mechanical instability.
SLOPE = SIGNAL BEHAVIOR A rising line means drift is intensifying. A falling line means a prior drift event is cooling. A level line means the signal is holding, not necessarily safe.
