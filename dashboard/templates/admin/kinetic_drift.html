Method Note

KDE does not claim direct Hawkeye/TrackMan ownership. It infers kinetic drift from available pitch-tracking variables, including velocity, induced vertical break, extension, release coordinates, and appearance-to-appearance movement deltas.

Waveform Color System

Color shows the dominant kinetic diagnosis. Shape shows whether the recent trace is accelerating, cooling, or holding.

Red = Decay / Fatigue Risk
Lime = Emergence / Power Gain
Cyan = Mechanical Instability
Reading The Trace

Color and shape are two different signals. A rising line means drift is intensifying. A falling line means a prior drift event is cooling. A level line means the signal is holding, not necessarily safe.

Metric Glossary
  • KDE Score: Composite drift read across risk, emergence, instability, and confidence.
  • KRS: Kinetic Risk Score. Breakdown/fatigue risk signal.
  • KES: Kinetic Emergence Score. Breakout, power, or stuff-gain signal.
  • KIS: Kinetic Instability Score. Release/mechanical volatility signal.
  • Confidence: Trust read based on sample strength and stability of the detected pattern.
  • Operator Command: Action posture for the asset: monitor, track, exploit, or avoid depending on drift profile.
