WAIVER WIRE // FIELD GUIDE

Open Market Protocol

Ownership Gate

The availability filter. Lower ownership means a wider acquisition window. Higher ownership means the market is already starting to absorb the asset.

Signal Window

The urgency band. 72H means move quickly. 96H allows monitoring. 7D is a slower stash window.

Asset Type

Separates Arms from Bats so the operator balances roster construction instead of blindly chasing every signal.

Market Defect

The reason the asset is still mispriced: ERA Lag, Role Fog, Command Discount, Role Block, Contact Discount, Call-Up Lag, or similar public-market hesitation.

Command

The roster behavior: Claim, Add, Stash, Track, or Monitor.

Risk

Exposure sizing. Medium can justify stronger action. High means volatility weapon, not blind acquisition.

Surface Strategy

Prioritize cards where the market defect is temporary, the signal window is short, and the command is stronger than public ownership implies. The edge is the mismatch between hidden physics and public hesitation.
